What you can do with Plagx Writing Companion

📊 Draft checks & reading time

Word, character, sentence and paragraph counts, average sentence length, Flesch Reading Ease score, and reading and speaking time estimates.

✍️ Grammar & style hints

Flags repeated words, possible passive voice, filler words, common mistakes like "could of", contractions, subjective phrasing, spacing problems and overly long sentences.

📚 Citation generator

Formats books, journal articles and websites in APA, MLA, Chicago and Harvard styles, with support for multiple authors, DOIs and accessed dates.

🔗 Reference matching

Compares in-text citations with your reference list, so missing or unused references are caught before you submit.

How it works

  1. Choose a tab: Draft Checks, Citations or Reference Matching.
  2. Paste or type your text, or fill in the source details.
  3. Review the results, which update instantly as you type.
  4. Copy and fix: copy citations into your bibliography and apply the hints to your draft.

Everything is done by JavaScript running in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent to a server, so the tool is safe to use with unpublished work. The hints and citation formats are rule-based, so always double-check them against your institution's style guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is Plagx Writing Companion free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account, limits or ads.

Is my text private?

Yes. All processing happens locally in your browser and your text is never uploaded.

Which citation styles are supported?

APA, MLA, Chicago and Harvard, for books, journal articles and websites.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes about 238 words per minute (average adult silent reading). Speaking time assumes about 130 words per minute.

Does this tool check for plagiarism or AI-generated text?

No. It is a writing helper only. To check complete documents for plagiarism and AI-generated text, use Plagiarism Checker X.
