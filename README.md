# Plagx Writing Companion

A free, browser-based academic writing utility:

- **Citations**: APA, MLA, Chicago and Harvard for books, journal articles and websites
- **Draft checks**: word, sentence and paragraph counts, readability score
- **Grammar & style hints**: repeated words, passive voice, filler words, contractions, spacing, long sentences
- **Reference matching**: finds in-text citations missing from the reference list, and references that are never cited
- **Reading time**: reading and speaking time estimates

Everything runs locally in your browser. No text is uploaded and no account is needed.

**Live:** https://plagiarismcheckerx.github.io/plagx-writing-companion/
